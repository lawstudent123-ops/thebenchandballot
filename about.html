About The Bench and Ballot

A platform for discussion, debate and exploring ideas.

About the Creator

My name is Anushka Bhasin, and I created The Bench and Ballot to share my passion for law, politics and economics.

I designed this platform as a space where people can write, share and discuss their ideas on important issues that shape society.

My interest in law, politics and economics inspired me to create a place where different perspectives can be explored through thoughtful articles and meaningful conversations.

The Bench and Ballot aims to encourage curiosity, critical thinking and a deeper understanding of the world around us.

Our Mission

The goal of The Bench and Ballot is to create a community where people can explore topics including law, politics, economics and society.

By sharing research, opinions and discussions, users can learn from one another and engage with different viewpoints in a respectful environment.
